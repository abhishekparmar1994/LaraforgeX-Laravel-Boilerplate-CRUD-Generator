પરિચય અને વ્યાપ (Introduction & Scope)

This Privacy Policy outlines how SolarPro & SolarSass Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (operating under the trade names "SolarPro & SolarSass" and "SolarTrack", reachable at https://solartrackpro.vercel.app/) collects, manages, processes, stores, and protects personal and commercial information collected through our web applications, customer tracking portals, mobile-responsive tools, and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

Our Platform is tailored specifically for the rooftop solar industry in India—including Solar Installers, EPC Contractors, Channel Partners, System Integrators, Solar Sales Representatives, and Survey Engineers managing projects from residential PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana installations to large commercial & industrial (C&I) solar plants.

By accessing or registering for our Platform, initiating a trial, or utilizing our customer tracking features, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of our Platform immediately.

2

અમે કયો ડેટા એકત્ર કરીએ છીએ (Information We Collect)

To provide an end-to-end solar ERP and customer tracking suite, we collect and process several categories of data:

A. Installer Account & Profile Data

  • Full Name, Registered Business/Entity Name
  • Official Business Email Address & Mobile Phone Number
  • GSTIN Number & PAN (for GST compliant invoicing)
  • Corporate Address, State, and District of operation
  • Role credentials (Owner, Admin, Surveyor, Sales Agent)

B. End-Customer & Rooftop Feasibility Data

  • Homeowner / Consumer Full Name & Contact Number
  • Site Address, Pincode & GPS Geo-Coordinates
  • Roof dimensions, roof type (RCC, Tin, Tile), tilt angle, shadow analysis
  • DISCOM Name (UGVCL, DGVCL, MGVCL, PGVCL, Tata Power, Adani, etc.)
  • Consumer Connection Number & Monthly Electricity Consumption

C. PM Surya Ghar & Subsidy Workflow Records

  • National Portal Application Reference Number
  • Uploaded verification documents (Electricity bill PDF, Aadhaar mask)
  • Feasibility approval status and Net Metering token reference
  • Work execution reports, inspection photos & Commissioning certificates
  • Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) subsidy disbursement status

D. Financial & Technical Telemetry

  • Solar Quotations, Bill of Materials (BOM), Invoices
  • Customer payment milestones (Advance, Structure, Commissioning)
  • Transaction reference codes (We do not store raw credit/debit card numbers)
  • Device IP address, browser type, timestamp logs, and error telemetry

ડેટાનો ઉપયોગ અને હેતુ (Purpose of Processing)

We process data strictly for lawful business objectives and legitimate contractual performance:

Solar ERP Pipeline Execution: Enabling installers to progress leads across survey, structure design, quotation approval, installation, net-metering, and subsidy release.
Automated Consumer Communication: Dispatching WhatsApp status updates and live customer tracking links so homeowners receive transparent progress without calling the office.
GST Invoice & Quotation Generation: Generating professional GST-compliant estimates and tax invoices displaying installer company letterheads.
Platform Security & Audit Logs: Preventing unauthorized access, enforcing Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and maintaining compliance logs under Indian cyber laws.

મલ્ટી-ટેનન્સી અને ડેટા ગોપનીયતા (Multi-Tenant Isolation)

Solar businesses operate in a competitive market. We treat customer lists, vendor pricing, and project files with zero tolerance for cross-exposure:

The Multi-Tenant Isolation Guarantee:

Every installer account is provisioned with a unique, cryptographically segregated Tenant ID. All database queries, cached records, and file storage buckets enforce strict tenant scoping.

Installer "A" in Surat or Ahmedabad can NEVER see or access the customer leads, financial margins, or site surveys belonging to Installer "B" in Rajkot or Vadodara.

ડેટા એન્ક્રિપ્શન અને સુરક્ષા પગલાં (Security Standards)

We implement multi-layered administrative, technical, and physical safeguards:

Encryption in Transit

All communications between your browser, mobile app, and our cloud servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / SSL 256-bit).

Encryption at Rest

Database tables, uploaded customer files, and sensitive records are stored encrypted using Advanced Encryption Standard (AES-256).

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

You determine whether field technicians, sales agents, or accounts personnel can access customer phone numbers, invoices, or profit margins.

Automated Backups & Failover

Point-in-time automated backups ensure zero business data loss even in the event of hardware or regional provider interruptions.

ડેટા જાળવણી અને ખાતું ડિલીટ કરવું (Data Retention)

We retain customer and project records as long as your subscription is active to enable ongoing project warranty tracking, subsidy dispute handling, and historical performance reviews.

Subscription Termination: If you cancel your subscription or trial, you have a 30-day grace period during which you can download full data backups in Excel / CSV / PDF formats.

Right to Erasure (Purge): Upon formal written request from your registered company email to solartrackpro@gmail.com, all your tenant database entries, consumer files, and quotation histories will be irreversibly deleted within 45 days, except where retention is strictly mandated by Indian taxation or GST record-keeping laws.

DPDP Act 2023 હેઠળ તમારા અધિકારો (Your Rights)

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023) and the Information Technology Rules of India, all users and data principals enjoy the following fundamental rights:

1. Right to Access & Summary: You can request a summary of your personal data processed on the platform and third parties with whom data has been shared.
2. Right to Correction & Updation: You can rectify inaccurate, incomplete, or out-of-date personal information directly in your profile settings.
3. Right to Grievance Redressal: You can file a grievance with our designated Grievance Officer and receive an official resolution within statutory timelines.
4. Right to Nominate: You have the right to nominate any individual to exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

કુકીઝ અને લોકલ સ્ટોરેજ નીતિ (Cookies Policy)
